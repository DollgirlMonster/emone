How to read it:
  occ % on base well under 90 -> prefill is waiting on the SSD here, unlike the M3;
     s256 and a >4096 chunk are the levers to build on.
  c2048 much slower than base -> prefill cost tracks the chunk count;
     an 8K/16K chunk (needs code: PrefillRuntimeConfig.maxChunkTokens) should pay.
  s256 faster with swap +0 -> run with --expert-cache-slots 256 (or --ram-budget) now.
  nobound faster -> the page-cache trade pays on this machine (TINYTITAN_BOUNDED_IO=0).
  c2048 may legitimately differ in output (different chunking); the others should not.
Spike 2:
  coalesce MUST read "same as base"; if it does not, that is a bug, not a trade.
  qqmm/hcfused/qsagpu may differ; a faster arm that differs needs a quality check
     (benchmark/quant_perplexity_ab.py) before it becomes a default.
  split is a diagnostic: read its prefill_split_* roles in r*-split.log, not its time.
Spike 3:
  mppwide/all may differ from base (different summation order); faster AND
     close is the bar, then benchmark/quant_perplexity_ab.py before a default.
  gqa MUST read "same as base": it keeps the per-head arithmetic exactly.
  Read GPU work (Gcycles) first; prefill seconds move with the clock.

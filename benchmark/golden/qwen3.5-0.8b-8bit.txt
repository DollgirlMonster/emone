### What is a Mutex?

A **Mutex** (short for **Mutual Exclusion**) is a synchronization primitive used in programming to ensure that only one thread can access a shared resource at a time. It prevents **race conditions**, where two or more threads might try to modify the same data simultaneously, leading to unpredictable or incorrect results.
